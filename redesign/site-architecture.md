# Site Architecture

The homepage should be built section by section. Do not try to complete the full website in one pass unless explicitly asked.

The current architecture is organized as chapters. These are conceptual chapters, not necessarily visible labels.

## Chapter 1: Now

Purpose: establish professional credibility and current identity.

This includes:

- Opening line
- Peiyu Chen
- Current position
- Institutional affiliation
- Research areas
- Quick links
- A possible concise news or update element, either inside or directly after the hero

The first impression should reassure academic readers quickly.

## Chapter 2: Questions

Purpose: introduce the questions Peiyu keeps returning to.

This should not be a generic "Research Interests" section. It should be organized around substantive questions.

Possible framing:

- "The questions I keep returning to."
- "What changes when technology mediates judgment?"
- "How do institutions make AI legible, accountable, or ordinary?"

The exact questions should be refined from Peiyu's work and writing, not invented as decorative copy.

## Chapter 3: Works

Purpose: present research, writing, and projects.

Works should eventually be organized around questions rather than separated mechanically into Research, Writing, and Projects. A reader should understand how each work participates in Peiyu's larger intellectual map.

Possible content types:

- Current research
- Selected writing
- Projects
- Policy commentary
- Talks or public-facing work

## Chapter 4: Formation

Purpose: explain what shaped Peiyu's research taste.

This is not an "About Me" section and not a memoir. It should answer:

"What shaped my questions?"

Formation should discuss experiences only insofar as they changed how Peiyu thinks.

Possible materials:

- Teaching
- China
- Chicago
- Books
- Space and the Micius satellite
- Public interest work
- Technology reaching ordinary people

Avoid sentimental storytelling. Use selective detail and intellectual consequence.

## Chapter 5: Looking Forward

Purpose: show current open questions, future directions, and intellectual movement.

This is not a generic career-goal section. It should feel like the continuation of a research agenda.

## News / Recent Updates

News is the site's living timeline or heartbeat. It does not need to be a full chapter.

Examples:

- New essay published
- Started a new research role
- Presented a project
- New article or policy commentary
- Updated CV

News should be concise, academic, and easy to maintain. Avoid promotional language.

## Navigation Model

Top navigation may include:

- Peiyu Chen
- Research
- Writing
- News
- CV

Navigation should be visually quiet. It should orient the reader without overpowering the opening sequence.

Preferred section labels include:

- Now
- Questions
- Current Work
- Selected Writing
- Formation
- News

Avoid overusing:

- About Me
- Portfolio
- Journey
- Story
